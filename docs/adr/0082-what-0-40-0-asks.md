# ADR 0082: What guideline 0.40.0 asks

**Status**: accepted (2026-09-29).

## Context

Tadas moves its pin from guideline v0.39.0 to v0.40.0. It is the first
move made as a merge. Tadas's `scaffold` branch holds the guideline's
scaffold rendered as `tadas`: first at v0.39.0, the release Tadas
pinned, which the main branch merged without changing a file, and then
at v0.40.0. So every difference between the two at v0.39.0 is Tadas's
own, and the move brings in only what the scaffold changed since.

The release names two reversals, and turns five of the scaffold's
recorded departures from `core` sections into the rules they departed
from. Tadas recorded all five as deviations of its own. Here is where
Tadas stands on each rule the release changes:

| Rule | What v0.40.0 asks | Here |
|------|-------------------|------|
| How to Read This | A tag covers the text under its heading up to the next heading of any level, so a `##` tag never reaches the `###` sections under it. | Follows, as it did. The Tag column of the deviations table already reads each `###` section by its own tag. |
| OM-12, Identifiers; STO-06 | A record a second run must find, what an outside delivery creates or what an orchestration step makes, takes `derived_id(key, at, part)`. Every other id is `new_id()`. | Follows. A task `/tadas add` creates takes `derived_id()` from the Slack delivery's key ([ADR 0027](0027-a-task-slack-creates-takes-an-id-derived-from-the-delivery.md)), and so does a row of an import stepped twice. ADR 0027 leaves the deviations. |
| OM-16, OM-02, Namespaces as Swimlanes (reversed) | No `audit` namespace from the start: an audit entry is an `Event` with an audit kind in the org's stream, until audit gains a reader of its own or must be kept longer than the stream. | Follows. That is [ADR 0011](0011-audit-entries-are-events.md), which leaves the deviations. |
| NET-22, Realtime at the Edge; DEL-18, Exceptions | The stream keeps a retention and a floor, and a read below the floor is `410 stream_truncated`, naming the floor and the head. An exception no shape fits sets its own status and code under the root. | Follows. `StreamTruncated` answers 410 with the floor and the head ([ADR 0040](0040-the-event-stream-has-a-floor.md)), which leaves the deviations. An event carries the request and the app, as the record now lists. |
| STO-34, Database Roles | Payloads and events, an audit entry among them, carry ids and never a personal value, so an erasure has nothing to redact in them. | Follows, as it did ([ADR 0026](0026-what-0-31-0-leaves-as-a-choice.md)). |
| STO-32, Database Roles | A person's account may be deleted at once, in one write, beside the soft delete purged after its retention. What they made in a team org stays the org's, under their id. | Follows. `delete_person` is that write ([ADR 0041](0041-an-account-is-deleted-at-once-and-its-providers-by-the-queue.md)). ADR 0041 stays a deviation from CTX-34 alone, for the unassignment on the service role. |
| CTX-27, Stages (reversed) | The recheck's setting is `realtime_recheck_seconds`, a setting of the realtime service, not `session_recheck_interval`. | Follows. That is the third decision of [ADR 0067](0067-what-0-36-0-asks-and-what-stays-a-choice.md), which is no longer a deviation. |

The release also bounds the loops of the operational skills again, and
the merge brings those bounds into `.agents/skills/`. Most of them Tadas
already held.

`make check` runs `arch-check` at v0.40.0, and it passes: 106 rules over
352 files, no finding, and 7 accepted by an exception. The same five
entries of `[[tool.arch-check.exception]]` accept them as at v0.39.0.

## Decision

**The move is a merge of the scaffold.** The pull request that carries
it merges with a merge commit, never a squash, so the render it merged
stays the base of the next move.

**Five deviations leave the table.** ADRs 0011, 0027, and 0040 are the
rules now, and their rows go. ADR 0041 follows STO-32, and its row keeps
CTX-34 alone. The third decision of ADR 0067 follows CTX-27, and its row
keeps OPS-11 alone. Each status line names this release.

**Nothing of v0.40.0 is a deviation.**

## Consequences

- The next pin move merges the `scaffold` branch again, from the
  v0.40.0 render.
- A new record that an outside delivery or an orchestration step
  creates takes `derived_id()`. Every other id is `new_id()`.
- An audit namespace of its own comes when audit gains a reader of its
  own, or must be kept longer than the stream.
