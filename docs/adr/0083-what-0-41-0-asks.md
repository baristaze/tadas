# ADR 0083: What guideline 0.41.0 asks

**Status**: accepted (2026-09-30).

## Context

Tadas moves its pin from guideline v0.40.0 to v0.41.0, by a merge of
its `scaffold` branch. The branch holds the guideline's scaffold
rendered as `tadas`, at v0.40.0 and then at v0.41.0, so the merge
brings in only what the scaffold changed between the two.

The release changes no section of the guideline. It reverses one lens,
and it adds the skill this move runs. Here is where Tadas stands on
each:

| Rule | What v0.41.0 asks | Here |
|------|-------------------|------|
| ASY-25, The Work Queue (reversed) | The manager's copy of an enqueue keeps the id as constructed and stamps the times, with the actor, the status, and the attempts. v0.40.0 held that the copy keeps the timestamps. | Follows, as it did. `enqueue` stamps the clock over the caller's times, which [ADR 0081](0081-what-0-39-0-asks.md) recorded when CON-17 asked it of every create. |
| Adopting, Upgrade a copy of the scaffold | A copy keeps its base on its `scaffold` branch, and takes a release by merging that branch, in a pull request that merges with a merge commit. | Follows. This move is `arch-upgrade-scaffold v0.41.0`. |

What the scaffold changed is text: its docs, its ADRs, three
docstrings, the two operational skills `ops-watch` and
`ops-investigate` with the tests that pin their counts, the version of
`astral-sh/setup-uv` in its workflows, and the local TOTP key. Most of
it is text Tadas held first and the scaffold lacked: a fact about the
core, or a path an ADR named wrongly. The scaffold holds it now, in its
own words.

`make check` runs `arch-check` at v0.41.0, and it passes: 106 rules over
356 files, no finding, and 7 accepted by an exception. The same five
entries of `[[tool.arch-check.exception]]` accept them as at v0.40.0.

## Decision

**The move is a merge of the scaffold.** The pull request that carries
it merges with a merge commit, never a squash, so the render it merged
stays the base of the next move.

**Where the scaffold holds the same fact, Tadas takes its words.** A
line that says the same thing in other words is a conflict at every
later move, and it is no product. So Tadas takes the scaffold's line on
each of these, in an ADR of its own too:

- No article stands before the name: [ADR 0001](0001-root-package.md),
  [ADR 0063](0063-sessions-last-weeks.md), the WorkOS runbook, and the
  first-time manual.
- The local TOTP key in `.env.example` is the scaffold's, which holds
  no name.
- Step 5 of `ops-watch` says where a query holds its period: its
  `Period`, and the last argument of a `SEARCH` expression.
  `get-metric-data` has no `--period`. The two strings of
  `infra/tests/test_ops_skills.py` that pin the sentence say the same.
- The two m6g prices of `deployment/cloud/README.md` are PostgreSQL's:
  $116 for `db.m6g.large` and $232 for `db.m6g.xlarge`.
- Three sentences leave the READMEs, since the scaffold states each
  elsewhere: what replicas share, that a namespace the image does not
  host refuses the boot, and which WorkOS application serves the local
  stack.

**What stays different is Tadas's.** Its product: tasks, billing,
Slack, the plans, and what each adds to a page the scaffold also has.
Its history: the date and the status of each ADR. And what stays out by
design: its migration chain, the scaffold's ADRs whose numbers Tadas
uses for its own, and the files it regenerates.

**Nothing of v0.41.0 is a deviation.** The deviations table is as it
was.

## Consequences

- The next pin move merges the `scaffold` branch again, from the
  v0.41.0 render.
- A generic fact written here alone is a difference the next merge has
  to resolve. In the scaffold, it comes back with the next move.
- A local database whose operator enrolled a second factor under the
  old TOTP key does not open that secret under the new one. The
  operator enrols again, or `.env` keeps the old key.
